## 1. Source answer key looks wrong (already changed in the app)

| Deck | Q | Question (short) | Source key | App now shows | Why |

## 2. Questionable (app keeps the source key answer)

| Deck | Q | Question (short) | Answer | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ch 5 | 15 | Cold water valve to the water heater shall not ___ | B (serve any other fixture) | §503.1 says the valve must not interfere with or cut off the supply to any other part of the system; "serve any other fixture" is a loose paraphrase. |
| Ch 8 | 16 | Laundry tray may discharge to the washer standpipe provided its waste line ___ | B (is without a trap and connects directly) | D ("discharges to the standpipe") is also technically true. |
| Ch 10 | 7 | Trap seals subject to evaporation are protected by a primer or a ___ | D (barrier-type device) | Older code editions also allowed a deep-seal trap (option A), which can be confusing. |
| 301–307 | 2 | Code vs. manufacturer's instructions conflict: the ___ requirements apply | C (more restrictive) | §301.7 literally says the **code** applies (option A), with an exception when the listing or instructions are more restrictive. C reflects the net effect; A is the literal wording. |
| 308–316 | 18 | 6-inch PVC drain needs ___ at a **45-degree** change in direction | B (sway bracing) | §308.6 requires sway bracing at changes **greater than** 45°, so an exact 45° turn arguably doesn't trigger it. |
| 412–426 | 21 | Head shampoo sink faucets require ___ | C (integral check valves) | Not sure of §412.10's exact wording; option A (limit to 110°F) may be the intended answer. Check the code book. |
| 606–613 | 14 | Private well distance from a septic tank | D (25 ft) | Matches IPC Table 608.18.1, but Virginia's private well regulations may require a larger setback. Check which rule your exam uses. |
| 701–707 | 22 | Caulking ferrules shall be ___ | A (copper alloy) | Bronze (B) and copper (C) are close variants. Check the exact §705.18 wording. |
| 701–707 | 25 | 3-inch branch, horizontal-to-horizontal fitting | D (wye) | Check the Table 706.3 footnotes: a short sweep (C) may also be allowed at 3 inches and larger. |
| 708–717 | 19 | Sump pit cover shall be installed ___ | B (≤ 2 in. below floor) | Not sure of the exact §712.3.2 cover wording. Check the code book. |
| 901–909 | 8 | Which city requires frost closure protection for vents | C (Goodland, KS) | Depends on the Appendix D design temperatures (below 0°F). I can't verify the city values; check Appendix D. |
| Ch 11–14 | 21 | Gray water for flushing toilets/urinals requires ___ | D (disinfection) | Some code editions also require blue or green dye (C). Check the 2021 §1302 wording. |
